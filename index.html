About Me

I’m Ali Al Rida El Hawli, a game developer based in Melbourne, Australia, currently studying Games & Interactivity / Computer Science (Game Development) at Swinburne University.

I specialise in designing and implementing gameplay systems, AI, and level interactions using Unity (C#) and Unreal Engine (C++). I enjoy building prototypes that explore player agency, horror tension, and systemic game mechanics.

My work includes AI-driven horror maze games, FPS movement and parkour, narrative-driven interactive stories, and board game designs. I’m always iterating on my tools and workflows to make future projects easier to build and maintain.
