AI & Workflow

How I use generative AI tools like ChatGPT in my practice as a game developer, while making sure the core work, decisions, and learning remain my own.

My Principles

  • Ownership: All final code, designs, and gameplay decisions are reviewed, understood, and approved by me.
  • Learning first: I use AI to support my learning, not replace it. If I don’t understand something, I dig deeper instead of blindly copying.
  • Integrity: For uni work and assessments, I follow my university’s academic integrity rules and only use AI where it’s allowed, with proper acknowledgement when required.
  • Transparency: I’m open about where AI has helped (e.g. code refactors, writing polish, idea generation), especially in professional and academic contexts.

What I Do Myself

  • Designing game concepts, mechanics, and systems.
  • Writing core gameplay code and AI logic.
  • Debugging and profiling in the engine.
  • Balancing difficulty, pacing, and level layouts.
  • Final decisions about architecture and implementation.

How I Use Gen AI

1. Research & Explanation

I use AI to get quick explanations of concepts (e.g. design patterns, AI techniques, shader basics) and then test that understanding by implementing them myself in Unity or Unreal.

2. Brainstorming & Variations

For quests, level ideas, enemy behaviours, or UI flows, I use AI to explore options and edge cases. I then pick, adapt, or reject suggestions based on what fits the project.

3. Code Review & Refactoring

I sometimes paste small pieces of my own code to get feedback on clarity, structure, or performance. Any changes are re-written or integrated by me, and I make sure I understand why they work.

4. Writing Support

For reports, documentation, and portfolio text, I use AI to help with wording, formatting, and proofreading. The underlying content, ideas, and examples are my own, drawn from my actual projects.

5. Productivity & Checklists

I use AI to generate task lists, break down big features into smaller steps, and spot potential issues (e.g. save systems, edge cases in AI behaviour) before I implement them.

Important: Any AI-generated content in my projects is treated as a starting point, not a finished product. I always review, test, and customise it to match the design goals and technical standards of the project.
